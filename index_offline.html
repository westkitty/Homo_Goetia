    The Sigil Forge

    What this chamber does

    Sigilization is the art of translating a focused intention or desire into a geometric glyph. By stripping repeating letters and vowels (a traditional Chaos Magic technique), we compress the desire into its base components, mapping them modulo 12 onto planetary vectors to draw a unique geometric sigil.

    How to use it

    1. Type a present-tense intention (e.g. "I AM BOLD AND AUTONOMOUS") or use the Intention Presets.
    2. Watch the visualizer isolate the consonants in real-time.
    3. Choose a styling lattice and click Forge Sigil.
    4. Download the final transparent PNG to use in your focus rituals.

The Seven Tenets establish a modern framework for personal sovereignty, critical thinking, and social justice, making it an empowering ideology for LGBTQ+ communities.
