# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Queer practitioners, Solomonic ritualists, and esoteric occultists seeking a private, offline-first personal temple for ritual work, sigil forging, daemonological exploration, and Solomonic magic.

## Product Purpose
Homo Goetia (Infernal Temple) provides a private, client-side, browser-based occult sanctuary combining authentic Solomonic ritual mechanics (all 72 Goetic spirits & 44 Key Pentacles), sigil forging, soundscape mixing, censer smoke visuals, and grimoire journaling.

## Positioning
A 100% private, client-side occult hub with zero external telemetry or server dependency, uniting authentic Solomonic & Goetic grimoire mechanics with a powerful red-and-black masculine ritual aesthetic and offline PWA reliability.

## Operating Context
Personal ritual spaces, solitary meditation, focus and edge timing, sigil creation and charging, soundscape layering, private cipher journaling, and complete Goetic/Solomonic lookup.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Capabilities**: All 72 Goetic Spirits of the Lesser Key of Solomon with SVG sigils, All 44 Pentacles of the Greater Key of Solomon across 7 Planets, Interactive Sigil Forge, Alchemical Dice, Ritual Transmuter, Black Sun Astrolabe, Private Cipher, Blood Pact, Tarot Oracle, TST Philosophy, Censer Smoke, Acoustic Nodes, Soundscape Mixer, Focus & Edge Timer, Descent Journey, Grimoire Journal, Shadow Mirror, Charging Station, Dark Scryer.
- **Technical Constraints**: 100% client-side vanilla JavaScript/CSS/HTML architecture, zero third-party tracking, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) manifest and service worker integration.
- **Terminology**: Sanctum, Altar, Banishment, Sigil, Transmuter, Goetia, Cipher, Grimoire, Astrolabe, Solomonic Keys, Pentacles.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Homo Goetia: Infernal Temple
- **Voice**: Authoritative, ceremonial, masculine, monolithic, reverent, dark, empowering, and private.
- **Identity Assets**: Deep obsidian void background (`#050405`), dark monolithic iron surfaces (`#100b10`), bold infernal blood-red flame accent (`#d61c38`), burnished brass/gold (`#c8963e`), gothic display fonts (`Cinzel Decorative`, `Cinzel`), and modern geometric body font (`Outfit`).

## Evidence on Hand
- Full client-side web application in `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, and `icon.svg`.
- Offline build bundle generator (`build_offline.js`, `index_offline.html`).

## Product Principles
1. **Uncompromised Client Privacy**: Zero telemetry, zero server-side storage; all ritual logs and ciphers remain strictly inside the user's browser.
2. **Complete Solomonic Authenticity**: Include all 72 spirits of the Lesser Key and all 44 Pentacles of the Greater Key with procedural SVG illustrations.
3. **Imposing Red-Black Aesthetic**: Maintain a powerful, masculine, dark-luxury ceremonial tone with deep obsidian surfaces and intense blood-red flame accents.
4. **Restrained Purposeful Motion**: Implement smooth, state-explaining micro-interactions supporting `prefers-reduced-motion` and low-spec devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast dark mode with clear semantic HTML hierarchy, accessible focus rings, keyboard navigation support, and reduced-motion fallbacks for all CSS animations.
